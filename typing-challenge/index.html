Bronze, silver and gold targets

Bronze: at least 80% accuracy. Silver: at least 90% accuracy and 15 WPM. Gold: at least 95% accuracy and 25 WPM. WPM is based on five correct characters per word.

🏅 Earn your certificate

Finish a passage and meet the Bronze, Silver or Gold target to unlock a personalised certificate. Enter your name after qualifying, then save the certificate as a high-quality PNG image. It includes your achievement, challenge and results.

Tips for pupils

Sit comfortably, use both hands, look at the text rather than the keys, keep the correct fingers near the home row, and prioritise accuracy. Errors are counted against the corresponding passage positions.
